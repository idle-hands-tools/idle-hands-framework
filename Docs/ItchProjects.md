# New itch.io project drafts

The account owner created all six project pages. All six browser builds were uploaded
successfully on 2026-10-28, and Butler verified each `html5` channel as processed
and ready at version **1**.

Create pages under `thisisnozaku`, keep them in **Draft**, choose **Games → HTML**,
set pricing to free, and use a **1280 × 900** browser viewport with fullscreen enabled.
For each uploaded `html5` file, enable **This file will be played in the browser**.
Publishing remains separate from creating these drafts.

## Orbital Foundry

- URL slug: `orbital-foundry`
- Tagline: Build an orbital factory and assemble your way home.
- Tags: incremental, management, sci-fi
- Archive: `Builds/OrbitalFoundry-itch.zip`
- Upload target: `thisisnozaku/orbital-foundry:html5`

Turn raw ore into plates, wiring, circuits, and spacecraft components. Allocate machines, change recipes, and resolve production bottlenecks as you rebuild a stranded ship.

- Start with mining and smelting, then build specialized machines.
- Balance shared inputs and storage; active batches finish before recipe changes apply.
- Deliver the hull, avionics, and drive to launch.
- Replay with an alternate blueprint and permanent production efficiency.

## Lastlight Railway

- URL slug: `lastlight-railway`
- Tagline: Reconnect isolated settlements with a railway of your own.
- Tags: incremental, management, trains
- Archive: `Builds/LastlightRailway-itch.zip`
- Upload target: `thisisnozaku/lastlight-railway:html5`

Build a small railway network, configure train itineraries, and move supplies between settlements. Cargo belongs to each station, so delivering it is as important as producing it.

- Build track links and configure cyclic train itineraries.
- Set loading limits and choose where cargo unloads.
- Fulfill delivery contracts and reopen the mountain observatory.
- Replay with a different contract order and demand quantities.

## Marshlight Reserve

- URL slug: `marshlight-reserve`
- Tagline: Restore a wetland by balancing habitats and its food web.
- Tags: simulation, management, nature
- Archive: `Builds/MarshlightReserve-itch.zip`
- Upload target: `thisisnozaku/marshlight-reserve:html5`

Bring a damaged wetland back to life. Improve water, purity, and shelter, establish vegetation and herbivores, then introduce predators when the habitat can support them.

- Restore three habitat zones with a coupled four-species food web.
- Observe population trends and respond with habitat interventions.
- Recover from local extinctions through reintroduction.
- Maintain healthy populations for five simulated minutes to complete the reserve.

## The Thirteenth Bell

- URL slug: `the-thirteenth-bell`
- Tagline: Solve a clockwork mystery across repeating twelve-minute days.
- Tags: interactive-fiction, mystery, time-travel
- Archive: `Builds/ThirteenthBell-itch.zip`
- Upload target: `thisisnozaku/the-thirteenth-bell:html5`

At the thirteenth bell, the town returns to morning. Your memory survives. Investigate overlapping appointments, gather evidence, and find a safe way to release the town from its repeating day.

- Investigate four locations and five witnesses.
- Plan around appointment windows and limited time.
- Retain knowledge and discover faster investigation routes across loops.
- Find three endings, including the complete release of the town.

## Tidebound Exchange

- URL slug: `tidebound-exchange`
- Tagline: Trade your way from a harbor warehouse to a thriving exchange.
- Tags: simulation, management, trading
- Archive: `Builds/TideboundExchange-itch.zip`
- Upload target: `thisisnozaku/tidebound-exchange:html5`

Buy cargo, watch supply-sensitive prices, and fulfill civic contracts. Your own trades move the market, while warehouse capacity and standing orders shape what opportunities you can take.

- Trade four commodities across three unlockable markets.
- Preview trade totals including price impact and spread.
- Configure storage allocations and buy/sell limit orders.
- Supply the lighthouse through a forty-minute season of civic delivery windows.

## Letters from the Floodwall

- URL slug: `letters-from-the-floodwall`
- Tagline: Every reply shapes a city’s evacuation before the river rises.
- Tags: interactive-fiction, choices-matter, story-rich
- Archive: `Builds/Floodwall-itch.zip`
- Upload target: `thisisnozaku/letters-from-the-floodwall:html5`

As an emergency correspondence officer, read conflicting reports, decide whom to trust, and send replies that shape a city’s response to an approaching flood. A coordinated plan depends on the promises you make and the people who believe you.

- Read a three-chapter story with five correspondents and twelve evidence entries.
- Explore distinct council and neighborhood routes.
- Search correspondence, follow evidence references, and track unresolved promises.
- Reach four endings and replay from chapter checkpoints.
